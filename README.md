# Alive Maps NYC

Walking directions for New York City that trade a few minutes for parks, trees, old buildings and streets with life.

Pick a start and an end. The page shows three routes: Alive, Most alive, and Fastest.

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Street trees, landmarks and historic districts from NYC Open Data. Address search by NYC Planning GeoSearch.

## Alive Maps Sevastopol

The same app for Sevastopol lives in `sevastopol/` and opens at `/sevastopol/`. It covers the whole city boundary: the centre, the North Side, Inkerman, Balaklava and the hills.

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), from the OpenStreetMap France extract. Tree cover from the Global Canopy Height map by Meta and the World Resources Institute (CC BY 4.0). Place search by Photon (Komoot).

How it was built, and how to rebuild it, is in `build/NOTES.md`.
